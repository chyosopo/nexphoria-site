## What the site is (read from the HTML, the Webflow stylesheet and every page)
- A Webflow telehealth store: weight loss (compounded semaglutide and
  tirzepatide, sublingual drops, the branded GLP-1s), anti-ageing (NAD+,
  glutathione, sermorelin), sexual health (an oral tablet, a gummy), and a
  delivery technology page (PermeAct). Assessment on forms.alyverx.com with a
  token per product; a patient portal on portal.alyverx.com.
- Design: soft ivory ground (#FFFFFB), fresh green accent (#8EC331), deep
  forest ink (#2B3618), olive and light lime tints. Figtree for body, Source
  Serif Pro for headings. Large radii (32px cards, pill buttons), a 1328px
  container, cards on tinted panels.
- The menu: each top item (Sexual Health, Weight Loss, Anti-Aging) opens its
  own full-width panel; a rail on the left ("View all products" with a
  picture), the products on the right, each with a thumbnail and its name; a
  featured product with its price and "Get started" in the header. On a
  phone the same panels stack with a back button.
- Home: hero with a rating line and six claim chips; a products grid with
  tabs (All / Injectable / Injection-free), each card "Starting at $X/mo ·
  Learn more · Get started"; three steps ("Take our assessment / Get
  prescribed / Delivered in less than 48 hours"); the technology section with
  a founder; three "why choose us" tiles; benefits; a results claim; a
  weight-loss calculator; before-and-after testimonials; a comparison table
  against "Others"; a new-treatments carousel; FAQ; a closer ("Start your
  journey today. Whatever better looks like to you, we'll make it happen.").
- Product page: status chip, rating, name, "From $X/mo", "See if I'm
  eligible", four facts with icons, one paragraph, then the same three
  steps, benefits, testimonials, FAQ, closer.

## What we took
- The menu: Treatments and Protocols each open a full-width panel with a
  rail on the left (all medicines with a picture, find your medicine, blood
  testing, how it works) and the medicines on the right, grouped by goal,
  each with its vial, its name and its price. Protocols show their art and
  price. The same panels are an accordion in the phone drawer.
- The hero fact strip: six facts with an icon each (physicians, pharmacy,
  blood test, online, cold shipping, one price).
- The grid filter by how you take it: any form, injection, nasal spray,
  taken as needed, beside the goal tabs.
- "See if I'm eligible" as the wording of the health-questions button on
  product, protocol, goal and quiz pages and the closers. It says what the
  next step decides.
- The product card grammar (status chip, name, from-price, one button) was
  already ours.

## What we did not take, and why
- Ratings, review counts, "10,000 patients", "As seen in": we publish no
  testimonials or counts.
- Before-and-after testimonials and the "Alyve vs Others" table: no
  testimonials, nothing about the market.
- "Alyve members lose 11 to 20%" and the weight calculator ("you could lose
  33 lbs"): outcome promises. The trial results live on the product page as
  results of trials, never as a prediction for the reader.
- "Make today life changing", "Start my transformation", "we'll make it
  happen": persuasion.
- "No insurance required", "Injection-free": defensive negation; we say
  "self-pay, one monthly price" and "nasal spray".
- The palette and the serif: asked as three options on 2026-09-05; Chiya
  chose "Keep Porcelain & Navy." The look stands; the structure was taken.
