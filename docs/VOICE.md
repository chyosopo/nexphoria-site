This replaces the "institutional bank" register of law 3 for DISPLAY copy
(headlines, tile titles, closers, buttons). Body copy stays plain and
factual. The legal and safety clauses are untouched.

## The register

1. **Short. Declarative. Stopped.** Two to five words, a full stop. Two
   statements beat one clause. "LOSE THE WEIGHT. KEEP THE MUSCLE."
2. **Caps for the shout.** Headlines, tile titles and closers set in caps,
   heavy, tight. Body copy in sentence case.
3. **Second person, present tense.** "Built around your baseline."
   "Know what you're after?"
4. **Performance words are allowed as the goal's own words:** stronger,
   sharper, leaner, rested, harder, faster, deeper. They name what the
   reader is after; they are not a promise of a result.
5. **Their words, where they are true for us.** "Built around your
   baseline" (the dose is set from blood work: true). "Know what you're
   after?" (the catalog is by goal: true). "Don't slow down" (the goal of
   the longevity medicines: true as a goal).
6. **One button, one verb.** Shop. See. Answer.

## The guardrails (unchanged)

- A goal is a goal, not a guarantee. "Lose the weight" names the goal of a
  GLP-1; "you will lose 20 lb" is a claim and is out.
- "Prescribed, if appropriate" and "if prescribed" stay wherever they are.
- The FDA and compounding disclosures stay verbatim.
- No urgency, no discounts, no counts, no "free" (audit:voice still runs).
- No comparison with other companies.
