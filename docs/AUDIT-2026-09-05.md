# Full audit — plan, architecture, UI, UX (2026-09-05)

Chiya: "full audit, the plan and architecture, then implementation,
everything, especially the UI and the UX." Measured, not opined: every
route rendered at 1440 and 390 px with Chromium; headings, heroes, tap
targets, image weight, overflow and failed requests counted.

## The architecture, as it stands

- Vite + React 18 + wouter. 22 routes prerendered to dist/public, the
  route list single-sourced in script/genSitemap.ts (sitemap + prerender).
- Static host: Cloudflare Pages. Apex = nexphoria.com (production branch
  claude/cloudflare-agent-setup-x9vzvc); preview = preview-10k.
- Data: soloCatalog (26 SKUs), stacksCatalog (6 protocols), peptides.ts
  (categories), monitoring, biomarkerPanel, goalTeaching (lines and shouts),
  goalImages, hero. Derived: vial facts, pathway, milestones, evidence.
- Imagery: review/studio renders everything from the catalog (vial, nasal,
  capsule, ampoule, box, carton, kit; SKU squares at 1600 and 600 px; goal
  tiles; product tiles per goal; protocol panels). Photographs from the
  July library for people.
- Voice: docs/VOICE.md (the enhanced register). Law 3 amended.
- Gates: ten in package.json plus bundle and funnel; deploy-apex.yml runs
  them in CI and refuses a publish that fails.

## The plan (what the site is for, in order)

1. Land: the shout, three goal tiles, one action.
2. Learn: how it works in three tiles; what a peptide does in three frames.
3. Choose: every medicine, by goal, with the price; protocols as tiles.
4. Decide: the product page states what it does, what arrives, the first
   twelve weeks, the evidence, the blood, who should not, who prescribes.
5. Buy: the plan, the cart, the checkout, the health questions.
6. After: the box, the first dose, the week-12 panel.

Steps 1 to 4 are built in the tile grammar. Step 5 is the blocker: the
checkout still posts to /api/checkout, which the static host answers 405.
The Bask handoff is unbuilt. Nothing on this list matters more.

## The journey, page by page (UX notes)

- Home → catalog: the hero tiles open the catalog on the goal (?goal=).
  Verified: Sleep shows DSIP alone.
- Catalog → product: the goal tiles are the filter; the cards carry the
  price above the render, so the price is never a surprise on the product
  page.
- Product → plan: "Choose a plan" scrolls to the buy box; the mobile bar
  repeats the price and Add to cart.
- Cart → checkout: works locally; on the host the submit fails (finding 8).
- Every closer is the same tile with the same shout, so the last thing on
  every page is one action.
