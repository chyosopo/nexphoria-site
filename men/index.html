    The medical engine

    How a protocol is decided.

    1. 01

      Intake

      A structured history — goals, training, medications, prior labs. Read by a person, not scored by a form.

    2. 02

      Panel

      Baseline bloodwork across eleven systems establishes the biology a protocol has to answer to.

    3. 03

      Physician

      A board-certified, U.S.-licensed physician reviews both, prescribes only what the numbers justify — and can decline.

    4. 04

      Compounding

      If prescribed, a state-licensed 503A pharmacy prepares it under USP <797> and ships it cold-chain.

    5. 05

      Retest

      The same markers are re-drawn every 90 days. The physician reads the trend and adjusts, holds, or stops.

    Questions

    What to expect.

    Does the consultation cost anything?

    No. The intake and physician review carry no charge. You pay only if a physician determines a prescription is appropriate and you choose to proceed.

    Is a prescription required?

    Yes. Every compound is prescribed by a U.S.-licensed physician of Arora Health & Aesthetics, LLC after reviewing your intake and bloodwork. They can — and do — decline when a protocol is not appropriate.

    Where are the peptides compounded?

    In state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies, prepared per prescription and batch-documented. Nothing is stocked, resold, or shipped without a physician's order.

    How often is my bloodwork re-run?

    The same panel is drawn again every ninety days. Your physician reads the new trend against your protocol and adjusts — nothing continues on assumption.

    How many biomarkers are measured?

    99 markers across eleven systems at baseline, including a twenty-one-factor biological-age composite — the same panel a physician would order to read your endocrine, metabolic, and cardiovascular picture properly.
